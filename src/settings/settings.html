Welcome to DiscourseCopilot — 3 steps to get started

DiscourseCopilot summarizes long topics and answers questions with sources on any Discourse forum. Connect your own AI provider below — or finish setup in the side panel, which walks you through the same steps.

  1. 1Choose a provider. OpenRouter gives you many models with one key; Ollama runs on your computer.
  2. 2Paste your API key (or your local server URL). It stays in this browser.
  3. 3Pick a model, then Test Connection and Save Settings.
