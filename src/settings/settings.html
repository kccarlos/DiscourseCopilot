Ask the forum

How much the agent may do for each question you ask a forum.

Research depth

The agent decides what to search and read, one step at a time, until it can answer or runs out of steps. Deeper research finds more, but takes longer, uses more AI tokens, and sends more requests to the forum (always paced, one at a time). Each follow-up question gets the same budget again.

3–40. A step is one search, one topic read or one list of latest topics.
1–20 different topics opened per question.
5,000–60,000. Longer topics are trimmed to the opening post and the latest replies.

Reading topics

How much of a topic summaries and follow-up chat read.

Pages read per topic
pages
Each page holds 100 posts, read from the start of the topic. A limit (1–100 pages) makes very long topics faster, cheaper, and lighter on the forum; a topic past it is summarized from its first pages, and the summary says so.

characters
5,000–1,000,000, rounded to the nearest 5,000. How much of the topic is sent with each follow-up question (also adjustable in the side panel). More context helps on long topics but uses more tokens.
